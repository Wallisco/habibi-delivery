One surface carries the state

Deep green means you are earning. White means you are not. A driver can read their status from a phone on a handlebar mount without focusing on it.

Money is the largest thing on screen

The fee and the day's earnings are set at 44 to 50px. Nothing else competes. That is the number a driver actually opens the app for.

Bright green is rationed

The live accent appears on exactly one action per screen and on the earnings bar. Used everywhere it would stop meaning anything.

Every target is 58px or taller

Buttons, the code field, the decline row. Sized for a gloved thumb in a hurry rather than a designer's cursor.
